# Combat Directive (0 to 1)

Phalanx Duel is not a game of luck. It is a system of consequences. You use a standard 52-card deck to build a digital killzone. 

## The Prime Directives

To survive your first duel, you must internalize three laws of the battlefield.

### 1. The Grid is the Strategy
You operate on a **4x2 formation**. 
*   **Front Rank:** Your attackers. Only the front row can strike.
*   **Back Rank:** Your reserves. When a front card falls, the reserve immediately collapses forward.
*   **A hole in your line is a direct path to your Life Points.**

### 2. The Cascade is Inevitable
Damage in Phalanx Duel is deterministic. It flows.
*   Select an attacker from your **Front Rank**.
*   Target an enemy **Column**. 
*   Damage destroys the front card, then "carries over" to hit the back card.
*   If the back card falls, the remaining damage hits the opponent's **Life Points (LP)**.

### 3. Suits are Your Tools
We have weaponized the standard deck through **Suit Boundaries**.
*   ♦ **Diamonds** and ♥ **Hearts** (Shields): These reduce damage as it passes through the column or reaches the player.
*   ♣ **Clubs** and ♠ **Spades** (Weapons): These multiply damage during the cascade or upon hitting the player.

**Mastery starts with math, not luck.**
