# Getting Started (0 to 1)

Welcome to Phalanx Duel. If you have a standard deck of cards and a friend, or if you want to play a fast, deep strategy game in your browser, you are in the right place.

## What is Phalanx Duel?

Phalanx Duel is a deterministic, 1v1 tactical card battle. 

Unlike trading card games, there are no booster packs, no meta decks to buy, and no hidden information other than the cards currently in your hand. You and your opponent play on a shared grid, maneuvering standard playing cards as your army. 

Every move is visible. Every combat outcome is mathematically predictable. The game is won purely on positioning, timing, and reading your opponent.

## Why is it worth learning?

*   **Fast to Start:** The rules take minutes to learn. You can play your first match today without reading a rulebook.
*   **Deep to Master:** Because combat resolves predictably across columns, high-level play becomes a game of deep tactical foresight, trap-setting, and tempo control.
*   **Standard Deck:** It uses an unmodified 52-card deck. The complexity comes from the board, not from reading paragraphs of text on custom cards.

## The Minimum Rules You Need to Know

You don't need to memorize every interaction before playing. Understand these three concepts, and you are ready to begin:

### 1. The Battlefield (Columns and Rows)
You play on a **4x2 grid**. That means you have **4 columns**, and each column has a **Front Row** and a **Back Row**.
*   **Front Row:** Your active attackers and first line of defense.
*   **Back Row:** Your reserves. If a front card is destroyed, the back card immediately steps forward to take its place.

### 2. Attacking (The Cascade)
You always attack with a card in your **Front Row**. You target an enemy **Column** (not a specific card).
*   Damage hits the enemy's front card first.
*   If the damage is higher than the card's value, it destroys the card, and the remaining damage "carries over" to hit the back card.
*   If the back card is also destroyed, the remaining damage hits the opponent's **Life Points (LP)** directly.

### 3. Suits Matter (Shields and Weapons)
*   ♦ **Diamonds** and ♥ **Hearts** are **Shields**. They mitigate damage as it passes through the column.
*   ♣ **Clubs** and ♠ **Spades** are **Weapons**. They multiply damage when you attack with them.

That is enough to play.
