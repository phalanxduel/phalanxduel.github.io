# Analytical Mastery

The master player does not just predict the outcome; they optimize it. Mastery in Phalanx Duel is about managing resource efficiency and quantifying risk before the first card is played.

## 1. Damage Efficiency: The LP/Atk Ratio
Every attack represents a resource expenditure. Mastery requires you to track your **Damage Efficiency**—the ratio of LP damage dealt to the attack value committed. 
- High efficiency breaches the column cleanly.
- Low efficiency signifies "waste" that can be turned against you by an observant opponent.

## 2. Stability Analysis: Ace Protection
Understanding when your core defensive structures are "stable" is key. The `Engine Trace` and `Tactical Briefing` provide real-time status on **Ace Protection**. Mastery is knowing when your defensive formation is immune to destruction, allowing for more aggressive board positioning.

## 3. The Path to Optimization
The simulation environment is not just for viewing outcomes; it is a laboratory for tactical refinement. By iterating in the Combat Lab, you can identify patterns that minimize your exposure while maximizing your offensive pressure.
