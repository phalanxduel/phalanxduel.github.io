# Your First Match (0 to 1)

Welcome to the Phalanx Duel battlefield. This walkthrough will show you what actually happens during your first few turns.

## The Objective

Your goal is simple: Reduce your opponent's Life Points (LP) to 0, or destroy all of their deployed cards. 

## Step 1: The Setup

Both players draw a **Hand of 12 cards**.
*   You take turns placing one card at a time onto your **4x2 grid**.
*   Your goal is to build a wall of defense while positioning your strong cards for a counterattack.
*   Once your 8 board slots are full, you will have exactly 4 cards left in your hand.

![Deployment with Hand]({{ '/assets/images/tutorial/deployment-with-hand.png' | relative_url }})

*Pro Tip: Do not put your highest value card in the front row where it can be easily picked off. Hide it in the back row to create a "second wave" attacker.*

## Step 2: The Attack

The game alternates in turns. On your turn, you will declare an attack.

1.  **Choose your attacker:** Select a card in your **Front Row**.
2.  **Choose their defending column:** You cannot target a specific card; you must target the whole column.

![Attack Phase]({{ '/assets/images/tutorial/attack-with-hand.png' | relative_url }})

**Example of an Attack:**
*   You attack with a **10 of Spades** in your front row.
*   You target the opponent's column. They have a **3 of Hearts** in the front row and a **4 of Clubs** in the back row.

**The Cascade:**
*   Your 10 hits their 3 of Hearts. The 3 is destroyed (10 > 3).
*   The remaining damage is 7 (10 - 3 = 7).
*   That 7 "carries over" and hits their 4 of Clubs. The 4 is destroyed (7 > 4).
*   The remaining damage is 3 (7 - 4 = 3).
*   Because the entire column collapsed, that 3 damage hits the opponent's **Life Points (LP)**. 

Because your attacker was a **Spade**, that final LP damage is doubled! The opponent loses 6 LP.

## Step 3: Reinforcement

After combat resolves, the defending column is now empty. The opponent must use their **Reinforcement Phase** to deploy new cards from their hand into the empty slots.

![Reinforce Phase]({{ '/assets/images/tutorial/reinforce-phase.png' | relative_url }})

*   If they do not have enough cards to fill the front row, they leave a massive hole in their defense for you to exploit next turn.
*   At the end of the turn, players draw back up to their Hand Limit (4 cards).

## You Are Ready

That is the core loop: Deploy, Attack, Resolve the Cascade, Reinforce. 

As you play, you'll discover how the suit modifiers (Shields and Weapons) create dramatic twists. But for now, you know enough to begin.
