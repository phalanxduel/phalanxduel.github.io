# Foundations: The 0-1 Mechanics

To understand Phalanx Duel, you must first master the interaction between your cards and the board. Phalanx Duel is a game of geometric consequence.

## 1. Deployment: The Front and Back Rows
Your column consists of two ranks.
- **Front Rank:** Takes the initial impact of the attack.
- **Back Rank:** Provides reinforcement and defensive support.

## 2. The Cascade: Mechanics of Damage
When an attacker engages, the damage doesn't just subtract health—it **cascades**. 
- **Carryover:** If a Front Rank defender is destroyed, the remaining damage flows to the Back Rank defender.
- **Breach:** If all defenders are destroyed, damage flows to your Core Life Points (LP).

## 3. The Suit Shields
- **Diamonds (♦):** Absorb carryover damage.
- **Hearts (♥):** Prevent damage to your Core LP.
