  <h2 id="canonical-format">Start Here: The Canonical Duel Format</h2>
  <p><strong>Phalanx Duel</strong> is the canonical competitive format of the Phalanx system. Classic play uses a <strong>4-column, 2-rank battlefield</strong>, a <strong>4-card hand limit</strong>, and deterministic attack resolution with suit timing that rewards planning over guesswork.</p>

<section class="grid three" aria-label="Phalanx Duel Progression">
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Play</h2>
    <p>Learn the board, choose attacks from the front rank, and feel how overflow damage moves through a defending column.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Improve</h2>
    <p>Understand suit timing: Diamonds and Hearts mitigate, Clubs and Spades convert carryover into pressure.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Master</h2>
    <p>Read attack boundaries correctly, exploit pass pressure, and plan reinforcement around column collapse.</p>
  </article>
</section>
