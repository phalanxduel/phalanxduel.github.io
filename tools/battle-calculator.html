How to Read the Results

  • Carryover: Damage that survives destroying the Front Row moves to the Back Row.
  • Diamond Shields: Reduce carryover damage before it hits the next card.
  • Club Weapons: Double the carryover damage once, right after the first card is destroyed.
  • Heart Shields: Reduce the final damage that reaches your Life Points (LP).
  • Spade Weapons: Double the final damage that reaches the opponent's LP.

The boundary order is strictly: Shield -> Weapon -> Clamp.
