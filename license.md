Phalanx is designed to be an open system. We encourage the community to build, fork, and implement their own versions of the rules and engine.

A copy of the full license text is available in the main code repository.

## Open Implementations

We strongly encourage open implementations and community adaptations of the Phalanx tactical system. Whether you are building a new digital client, a tabletop tool, or a rules expansion, the open nature of the engine ensures you have the freedom to innovate.
